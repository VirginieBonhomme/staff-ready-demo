# Client Content Intake

Use these questions with a new restaurant before building their version.

## Basics

- What is the restaurant name?
- What are the operating hours?
- What roles work each shift?
- Which roles open?
- Which roles close?
- Who signs off checklists?

## SOPs

- What procedures must every employee know?
- What procedures are legally required?
- What procedures are high-risk if done wrong?
- Which procedures are different for BOH and FOH?
- What photos or diagrams should be included?

## Checklists

- What must be done before opening?
- What must be done before service?
- What must be done during service?
- What must be done before closing?
- Which items need manager sign-off?
- Which items require a log or form?

## Roles

- What does each role own?
- What SOPs does each role need?
- What checklists does each role use?
- What does success look like by the end of shift?
- What should new hires read first?

## Food Safety

- What logs are required?
- What temperature limits does the restaurant follow?
- What allergen process is currently used?
- What corrective actions should staff take?
- Who gets notified when something is out of standard?

## Crisis

- What emergencies are most likely?
- Who gets called first?
- Where are shutoffs located?
- Where are first aid supplies?
- What must staff do before calling a manager?

## Menu / Recipes

- Which recipes need specs?
- Which dishes have allergens?
- Which prep items need shelf-life rules?
- Which items are common sources of mistakes?
