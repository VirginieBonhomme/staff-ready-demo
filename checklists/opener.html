Opener Checklist

Role: Opener Updated: May 2026 Category: BOH Operations

The opener owns the kitchen before anyone else is in it. Food safety checks come first — before any prep begins. Work through every section in order, then submit the checklist before service starts.

1. Food Safety — Do These First

Temperature Checks

Calibrate probe thermometer using the ice-point method — must read 32°F ±2°F
Log fridge temperature — must be ≤ 41°F. If above 46°F, do not use food inside. Alert manager immediately.
Probe-check a dense item on the middle fridge shelf — core must also read ≤ 41°F
Log freezer temperature — must be ≤ 0°F. If above 5°F, notify manager before service.
Submit daily temperature log via the form below before service starts

Receiving (Delivery Days Only)

Inspect delivery vehicle and driver documentation before unloading
Check all packaging — reject anything torn, wet, or with compromised seals
Probe chilled goods — reject anything arriving above 46°F
Check all use-by dates — reject any item with missing or expired date
Store accepted goods FIFO — new stock behind old stock
Submit receiving log via the form link below
2. Date Label Audit
Check every labelled item in the fridge — remove and discard anything past its use-by date
Any unlabelled food found must be discarded immediately — do not guess the date
Re-label any items whose labels are illegible or missing a use-by date
Log any discards with item name and reason in the corrective action field on the temp log
3. Kitchen Setup

Hygiene & Safety

Wash hands before touching anything in the kitchen
Handwashing sink stocked — soap, paper towels, running hot water confirmed
Blue detectable plasters available at the handwashing sink
Sanitise all cutting boards and prep surfaces before use
Test sanitizer solution with test strip — log result; chlorine 100–200 ppm, quat 200–400 ppm

Equipment

Start fryer preheat — target 350°F before service
Confirm probe thermometer is available at the pass and sanitised
Confirm all knives are on the magnetic strip — none left on surfaces
Check bin liners are in place in all bins

Prep & Stock

Pull protein for first service — confirm it is within date and correctly stored
Set up garnish station with labelled, dated items
Check dry storage — nothing on the floor, FIFO rotation in place
Confirm pass is clean, clear, and ready
4. Team Handoff
Brief each station on their prep priorities and first service deadlines
Communicate the 86 list, allergen flags, and today's specials to all kitchen staff
Confirm fryer has reached 350°F before first ticket is called
Escalate any equipment issues, missing stock, or food safety concerns to manager before service
Submit Checklist

Submit before service starts. The form is timestamped and logged automatically in the manager's Google Sheet.
