Daily Temperature Log

Role: Opener / Line Cook / Closer Updated: May 2026 Category: Food Safety Record

Complete all your checks, then submit via the form below. Every submission is automatically timestamped and saved to the manager's Google Sheet — no paper required.

Have These Readings Ready Before You Submit
Thermometer calibration result — ice-point reading (°F) and pass/fail
Fridge temperature(s) — opening, mid-service, and closing
Freezer temperature — opening reading
Hot hold temperature — if in use
Food probe check — item probed and core temperature
Cook and reheat temperatures for any proteins cooked during service
Cooling records — start time, 2-hour reading, and final temperature for any batch cooled today
Any corrective actions taken — what failed and what you did
