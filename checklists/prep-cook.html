Prep Cook Checklist

Role: Prep Cook Updated: May 2026 Category: BOH Operations

Prep drives service. Everything you produce must be labelled the moment it leaves your hands, stored correctly, and ready on time. Work through this checklist at the start and end of every prep shift.

1. Prep Station Setup

Hygiene First

Wash hands before touching any food or equipment
Sanitise all cutting boards and prep surfaces before setting up
Confirm colour-coded boards are correct — red for raw red meat, yellow for poultry, blue for fish, green for produce, white for dairy and cooked
Set sanitizer spray bottle and paper towel at station — test strip confirms correct concentration

Stock Audit

Pull today's prep list from manager before touching any product
Inspect all prepped items from yesterday — check label, date, and condition
Discard anything past its use-by date — do not re-label or guess
Rotate new stock FIFO — new deliveries go behind existing stock
Flag any low stock, missing product, or quality issues to manager before starting prep

Station Equipment

Scale is zeroed and clean
Deli cups, labels, permanent marker, and lids are at the station
Probe thermometer available and sanitised for checking hot prep
2. During Prep

Labelling — No Exceptions

Label every prepped item the moment it is finished — item name, prep date, use-by date
Write labels in permanent marker — legible and on the container, not the lid
No unlabelled containers in the fridge, ever

Cooling Hot Prep

Never put hot food directly into the fridge — always ice-bath first
Use shallow containers no deeper than 3 inches
Cool from above 140°F to below 70°F within 2 hours, then to 41°F or below within 4 hours total
Probe the centre of cooling batches — log the reading on the daily temperature log
If food has not reached 41°F within 4 hours, discard it and notify manager

Cross-Contamination

Sanitise board and knife between each protein type
Never use the same board for raw protein and produce without sanitising first
If an allergen-free batch is required, use the purple dedicated board and clean utensils
Wash hands every time you switch between protein types
3. End of Prep Shift
All completed prep is labelled, covered, and stored in the correct fridge location
Any remaining hot product is ice-bathed and cooling correctly before you leave
Sanitise all prep surfaces and boards
Return all equipment — scales, containers, deli lids — to their storage locations
Note any prep items not completed and tell the manager or opener before leaving
Submit Checklist

Submit at the end of your prep shift. Logged automatically in the manager's Google Sheet.
