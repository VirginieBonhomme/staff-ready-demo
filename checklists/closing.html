Closing Checklists

BOH & FOH Role Responsibilities Updated: May 2026

Use the right close for the right team. BOH closes food, equipment, dish, safety, and storage. FOH closes the room, guest systems, service stations, money, and handover notes. Role notes below remove ambiguity.

BOH Role Closing

Kitchen role responsibilities

Line Cook

Label, date, cover, and store station mise; discard expired or unsafe food
Clean grill, fryer exterior, hot line, cutting boards, pans, utensils, and station surfaces
Turn off assigned equipment and hand over low stock, prep needs, or repairs

Prep Cook

Wrap and label all prep, update prep list, and rotate product into correct storage
Clean prep tables, scales, slicer or processor parts, sinks, labels, and storage bins
Write tomorrow's prep priorities and flag shortages before leaving

Dishwasher

Run final dish loads, delime or drain as required, clean filters, screens, racks, and sinks
Air-dry and store clean items; return smallwares to line, prep, bar, and service stations
Sweep, mop, empty bins, reline cans, and leave dish area dry and reset

Opener Ending a Shift

Leave opening notes for tomorrow: equipment flags, prep gaps, product quality, and deliveries
Confirm morning-only tasks were completed or handed over to the closer
Restock opening supplies if the team will need them first thing tomorrow

Closer

Own BOH closing checklist submission and confirm all kitchen role closes are complete
Log final fridge, freezer, waste, equipment, cleaning, and security checks
Write manager handover before locking up or leaving the kitchen
FOH Role Closing

Guest-facing role responsibilities

Server

Close all tables, check payments, file receipts, report comps, and finish assigned sidework
Reset section, polish cutlery/glassware, restock stations, and record guest issues
Hand over VIPs, complaints, allergies, lost property, and maintenance notes

Host

Reconcile reservations, no-shows, waitlist notes, future bookings, and special requests
Reset host stand, menus, table plan, coat area, entry, and waiting area
Record guest feedback, accessibility needs, and table pacing issues for manager review

Bartender

Close tabs, reconcile bar payments, clean wells, discard or label garnish, and secure stock
Restock glassware, napkins, straws, wine tools, mixers, and opening par where possible
Write low stock, breakage, keg, wine, batch, and maintenance notes
