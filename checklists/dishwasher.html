Dishwasher Checklist

Role: Dishwasher Updated: May 2026 Category: BOH Operations

A clean dish area is a safe kitchen. Your job touches every other role — the kitchen and FOH cannot run without clean wares moving at the right speed. Work through this checklist at the start and end of every shift.

1. Opening — Dish Machine
Fill machine and run a warm-up cycle before accepting any wares
Check chemical dispenser levels — rinse aid and detergent both above the minimum line
Confirm wash temperature is within the machine's required range
Check and clean all filters and drain screens — remove debris from previous shift
Inspect curtains and door seals — replace if torn or missing
Report any machine faults to manager before service starts
2. Opening — Three-Sink Area
Set up three-sink area: wash sink with hot water and detergent, rinse sink with clean hot water, sanitise sink with correct solution
Test sanitizer concentration with a test strip — chlorine 100–200 ppm or quat 200–400 ppm — and log the result
Set drying racks and air-dry area — no towel drying of food-contact wares
Set glass racks and cutlery bins in correct positions
Empty and clean all bus tubs from the previous shift before service
Stage clean smallwares in the correct storage location for prep cook use
3. During Service

Machine Operation

Keep racks moving — first in, first out — do not let clean wares sit in machine
Pre-scrape all wares before loading — no solid food into the machine
Allow all wares to air-dry completely before stacking or storing
Never stack wet dishes — bacteria can transfer between wet surfaces

Sanitizer Log

Test sanitizer solution every 2 hours — log result via the sanitizer log form
Discard and remake solution if concentration falls outside the acceptable range
Submit sanitizer log result immediately after testing — not at the end of your shift

Dish Area

Keep dish area floor dry — squeegee spills before they spread
Keep walkway between dish area and pass clear at all times
Alert manager if racks stop moving or clean wares are critically low
4. End of Shift
Run a final empty rack through machine to flush the interior
Drain machine and wipe interior — including door seals and spray arms
Drain and clean three-sink area — all three sinks rinsed and wiped
Clean drain screens and leave the drain clear
Sweep and mop dish area floor
Empty and reline all bins in dish area
Restock clean wares to correct storage locations
Submit sanitizer log for final test of the shift
Submit Checklist

Submit at the end of each shift. Logged automatically in the manager's Google Sheet.
