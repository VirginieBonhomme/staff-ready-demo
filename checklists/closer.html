Closer Checklist

Role: Closer Updated: May 2026 Category: BOH Operations

The closer owns the kitchen from last ticket to lock-up. The opening team should be able to walk in tomorrow without discovering a single problem. Work through every section before leaving and submit the checklist before you sign off.

1. End-of-Service Food Safety

Temperature Records

Log final fridge temperature — must still read ≤ 41°F
Log final freezer temperature — must still read ≤ 0°F
Any food not cooled to ≤ 41°F within 4 hours total must be discarded — log the discard
Submit the daily temperature log via the link below before leaving

Food Storage

Label and date every prepped item going back into the walk-in — name, prep date, use-by date
Cover all containers — no open food anywhere in the fridge
Raw proteins stored on the bottom shelf, covered and sealed
Discard any unlabelled food found at close — do not re-label and store
Return all knives to the magnetic strip — none on surfaces or in drawers
2. Equipment Shutdown
Scrape and clean grill while still hot — use heat-resistant gloves
Season grill grates after cool-down
Turn off fryer — do not leave on overnight
Turn off all ovens and hot-hold equipment
Confirm all pilot lights and gas valves are off
Confirm exhaust hoods are off after cleaning
3. Cleaning
Sanitise all prep surfaces and cutting boards
Clean and sanitise handwashing sink and prep sinks
Wipe down pass, shelves, and any horizontal surfaces
Sweep and mop kitchen floor — including behind equipment
Empty and reline all bins
Remove bin bags to the external waste area — bins must not be left overnight
Clean behind and under fryer (weekly — log when last done)
Floor drains are clear and free of food debris
4. Handover Notes
Note any equipment faults or maintenance issues for the opening team
Flag any low stock, missing deliveries, or labelling issues found at close
Note any food discards and the reason so the opening team and manager are aware
Leave handover notes where the opener will see them — pass or manager board
5. Security
Lock walk-in cooler
Lock dry storage room
Confirm all lights off in kitchen and storage areas
Set alarm and lock up
Submit Checklist

Submit before leaving the building. The form is timestamped and logged automatically in the manager's Google Sheet.
