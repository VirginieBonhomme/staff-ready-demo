Server Checklist

Role: Server Updated: May 2026 Category: FOH Operations

Your section is guest-ready when you can walk every table and find nothing wrong before service opens. Work through this checklist before doors open and submit at the end of your shift.

1. Opening — Section Setup

Your Section

Walk every table in your section — no wobbles, crumbs, stains, or uneven settings
Set tablecloths or mats, cutlery, glassware, and napkins to the house standard
Check chairs are clean, stable, and aligned
Confirm candles, condiments, and any table items are clean and stocked

Service Station

Stock water station — pitchers filled, glasses polished, backup napkins ready
Stock service station — pens, straws, trays, wine keys, extra menus
Confirm you have enough clean napkins and cutlery for your section par

POS & Payments

Log into POS and confirm table numbers match your section layout
Test printer routing — confirm it hits the correct kitchen station
Confirm payment terminal is charged and processing correctly
2. Pre-Service Brief
Read specials, including price, allergens, and estimated pickup time
Note the 86 list — know what you cannot offer before your first table is seated
Read all allergen flags in today's reservations — know which guests are coming with dietary needs
Know today's VIPs, special occasions, large parties, and any timing pressure points
Know the section pacing plan — how many covers and when to expect a rush
3. During Service

Allergen Protocol

When a guest declares an allergy, ask clarifying questions — do not assume
Enter the allergy in the POS on every course — do not rely on memory
Verbally confirm the allergy with the kitchen when placing the order
Announce the allergy dish aloud when delivering to the table
If unsure whether a dish is safe — do not guess. Check with the kitchen before serving.

Service Standards

Check back within 2 minutes of first food delivery at each table
Read every ticket before running food — confirm table number and item before leaving the pass
Keep your section clear — crumbed plates removed promptly, water topped, empty glasses cleared
Any guest complaint — listen, do not argue, act within your authority, escalate to manager if needed
4. End of Shift
Clear and reset all tables in your section — crumbs, wipes, glassware, condiments
Restock service station to opening par
Restock water station and confirm pitchers are clean and stored
Close all open tabs and confirm with manager before signing off
Report any unresolved guest issues, complaints, or incidents to the manager
Note any 86 items that need updating before next service
Submit Checklist

Submit at the end of your shift. Logged automatically in the manager's Google Sheet.
