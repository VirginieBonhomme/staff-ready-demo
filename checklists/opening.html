Opening Checklists

BOH & FOH Role Responsibilities Updated: May 2026

Choose the right opening checklist before service starts. BOH owns food, prep, equipment, and safety. FOH owns the room, guest systems, reservations, and service stations. Role notes below clarify who checks what.

BOH Role Opening

Kitchen role responsibilities

Line Cook

Confirm station is sanitised, stocked, labelled, and ready for first ticket
Check fryer, grill, hot-hold, utensils, towels, and backup pans before service
Read 86 list, specials, allergen notes, and expected rush timing

Prep Cook

Pull prep list, inspect yesterday's labelled items, and rotate stock FIFO
Set cold prep station with boards, sanitizer, labels, deli cups, and scales
Flag low prep, missing product, or quality issues before service starts

Dishwasher

Start dish machine, verify chemical level, temperature, filters, and drain screens
Set three-sink area, drying racks, glass racks, cutlery bins, and clean storage
Empty bus tubs from previous shift and stage clean smallwares for prep

Opener

Own BOH opening checklist submission and escalate blockers before service
Verify fridge, freezer, hot-hold, probe, hand sink, and first service setup
Confirm station owners know their priorities and first prep deadlines

Closer Starting a Shift

Review previous close notes for maintenance, low stock, cleaning misses, or handover flags
Confirm close-down supplies are stocked before service gets busy
Identify any end-of-night tasks that need early prep or manager approval
FOH Role Opening

Guest-facing role responsibilities

Server

Set section, water station, cutlery, napkins, trays, pens, and sidework par
Read specials, 86 items, allergens, VIP notes, and service pacing plan
Test POS login, printer routing, table numbers, and payment terminal access

Host

Confirm reservations, waitlist tools, seating plan, accessibility needs, and special occasions
Walk entrance, host stand, menus, restrooms, waiting area, and first impression details
Brief servers on allergies, VIPs, large parties, and table turn pressure points

Bartender

Stock ice, garnish, glassware, fruit, syrups, batch items, wine keys, and polishing cloths
Check bar POS, card terminal, receipt paper, tabs, and cash drawer setup
Confirm drink 86s, specials, low stock, and non-alcoholic recommendations
