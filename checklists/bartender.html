Bartender Checklist

Role: Bartender Updated: May 2026 Category: FOH Operations

The bar must be ready before the first guest order arrives — not during it. Every item on this list is your responsibility. Work through it at opening and close, and submit at the end of each shift.

1. Opening — Bar Setup

Stock & Garnish

Fill ice wells completely — bar cannot run without ice from the first order
Set garnish tray — citrus cuts, olives, cherries, herbs, and any prep specific to today's menu
Check batch cocktail items — correct label, within date, stored correctly
Stock fruit syrups, juices, and mixers — note any low stock before service
Confirm all glassware is polished and at the correct par level
Stock wine keys, polishing cloths, bar napkins, and stir spoons at every station

Equipment & Systems

Test all draft taps — pour, check clarity, and confirm pour temperature
Test espresso machine and confirm milk is stocked and cold
Test all soda guns and confirm CO2 pressure is adequate
Log into bar POS and confirm table and tab routing is correct
Test card terminal — confirm it is reading and processing
Check receipt paper — replace roll if low
Count and confirm cash drawer with manager before service opens

Service Brief

Confirm drink 86s — know what is unavailable before the first order
Know today's featured cocktail, wine recommendation, or non-alcoholic special
Review any allergy notes tied to bar items — nuts in cocktails, dairy in coffee, gluten in beer
2. During Service

Allergens

When an allergy ticket comes in — check the drink against the allergen list before making it
Use a clean glass and clean tools for allergen-sensitive drinks — do not reuse tools from other orders
Never assume a cocktail or modifier is free of an allergen — confirm with the recipe or manager

Bar Standards

Wipe bar top after every transaction — no spills, no empty glasses left by guests
Restock ice as needed throughout service — never let an ice well run below half
Restock garnish mid-service before it runs out — do not cut garnish during a rush
If a tap blows or a piece of equipment fails, notify manager immediately
3. End of Shift

Bar Closing

Drain and clean ice wells — no standing water overnight
Discard all cut garnish — do not hold overnight
Label and date any remaining syrups or juices — refrigerate correctly
Wipe and sanitise entire bar top, speed rail, and all contact surfaces
Restock glassware to par — polished and in correct position for opening
Discard any open bottles that cannot be resealed and log accordingly

Cash & Tabs

Close all open bar tabs — confirm no tabs left before reconciliation
Count cash drawer and reconcile against system — note any variance
Hand reconciliation report and any tab discrepancies to manager
Log any comps, voids, or manager overrides from the shift
Submit Checklist

Submit at the end of your shift. Logged automatically in the manager's Google Sheet.
