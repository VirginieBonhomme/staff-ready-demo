Host Checklist

Role: Host Updated: May 2026 Category: FOH Operations

The host is the first and last impression every guest has of the restaurant. The room must be perfect before a single guest arrives. Work through this checklist before doors open and submit at the end of your shift.

1. Opening — Room & Entrance

Front of House

Unlock and inspect guest entrance — no debris, stains, or obstructions outside
Host stand is stocked — menus, pens, reservation system, seating chart
Check coat area or waiting area is clean, clear, and ready
Walk every table — wobbles, crumbs, uneven settings, and dirty glassware all need fixing before service
Check lights, music level, and temperature — all set to house standard
Restrooms inspected, stocked with soap and paper towels, clean, dry, and logged before doors open

Menus

Menus are clean — no sticky covers, torn pages, or outdated inserts
Enough menus for expected covers plus 20% buffer
Any menus with allergen inserts or specials are updated for today
2. Reservations & Pre-Service Brief
Review all reservations — confirm names, party sizes, times, and table assignments
Flag VIP guests, special occasions, and birthdays — brief servers on each one
Read every allergy note in the reservation system aloud to the team in the pre-service brief
Identify accessibility needs — wheelchair access, hearing, seating preferences
Note any large parties and confirm with the kitchen on pacing and timing
Confirm the seating plan and section assignments with manager before service
3. During Service

Door & Seating

Greet every guest within 30 seconds of entering — eye contact, name if reservation known
Walk-ins are quoted accurate wait times — do not underquote to please
Pace seatings with the kitchen — do not flood sections back-to-back
Escort guests to the table — do not point and direct
Allergen guests: verbally confirm allergy with server at handoff — do not leave it to the guest to repeat

Floor Monitoring

Check restrooms every 30–45 minutes during service — restock and clean as needed
Monitor waiting area — update guests on wait times proactively
Alert manager immediately if a guest is becoming agitated or a wait is running significantly long
4. End of Shift
Reset host stand — menus restocked, system closed correctly, pens returned
Final restroom check — cleaned, stocked, and logged
Log any guest complaints, incidents, or accessibility issues for manager review
Note any large parties, VIPs, or special setups for the following day's opening team
Confirm tomorrow's reservations are visible in the system before leaving
Submit Checklist

Submit at the end of your shift. Logged automatically in the manager's Google Sheet.
