Health Inspection Prep Checklist

Manager Updated: May 2026 Category: Compliance

Use this checklist to confirm your kitchen is inspection-ready. Inspections can be unannounced — this should reflect your daily standard, not a panic response. Work through each section with your manager before opening. Items marked with a record requirement must have supporting paperwork available on request.

1. Documentation & Records
HACCP plan is documented, up to date, and accessible — view HACCP
Daily temperature logs are completed and filed for the last 3 months
Receiving logs are completed and filed for the last 3 months
Sanitizer concentration logs are completed and filed for the last 3 months
Corrective action records are on file with root cause and outcome noted
Thermometer calibration records are available for the last 3 months
Pest control contractor reports for the last 12 months are on file
Employee health policy is documented and all staff have been briefed on it
Allergen information is documented for every dish on the menu
Food handler certificates are current for all applicable staff
2. Temperature Control
All fridges reading ≤ 41°F — confirmed with probe and logged
Freezer reading ≤ 0°F and confirmed
Hot hold units reading ≥ 145°F before service
Probe thermometers calibrated today and log signed
Spare probe thermometers available and calibrated
All probe thermometers sanitised and in working condition
Cooling and reheating procedures are understood by all kitchen staff
3. Food Storage & Labelling
Every prepped item in fridge has a label with item name, prep date, and use-by date
No unlabelled food anywhere in the kitchen, fridge, or dry store
Fridge storage order is correct — raw proteins below ready-to-eat foods
Raw red meat is on the bottom shelf, poultry above, fish above that
All raw proteins are covered and sealed in containers
Dry store is organised, off-floor, and FIFO rotation is maintained
No out-of-date products anywhere in storage
Date labels are legible and written in permanent marker
4. Hygiene & Personal Health
Handwashing sink is accessible, stocked with soap, paper towels, and running hot water
Staff know when to wash hands and are doing so correctly — 20 seconds minimum
No staff on shift who have reported symptoms in the last 48 hours
Blue detectable plasters are stocked and available at handwashing sink
No jewellery, nail varnish, or false nails on food-handling staff
Clean uniforms on all staff — hair tied back or covered
Employee health policy understood — staff know who to call when sick
5. Allergen Management
All 9 major allergens are documented on each relevant recipe
Allergen handling SOP is in place and kitchen staff can demonstrate the process
FOH staff can accurately answer allergen questions and know to flag requests to the kitchen
Allergen-dedicated preparation equipment is available (dedicated board/utensils)
Any menu changes have been checked against allergen information
6. Cleaning & Sanitising
Sanitizer test strips are stocked and correct type for the sanitizer in use
Sanitizer solution has been tested today and result logged
Colour-coded boards are all present, clean, and stored in designated positions
Three-sink area is set up correctly with correct sanitizer concentration
Dish machine temperature and chemical indicators are within range
Floor drains are clean and free of debris
Cleaning schedule is posted and signed off for the current week
7. Pest Control
No evidence of pest activity found during today's opening check
Most recent pest control report is on file and action items are addressed
External waste area is clean, bins are sealed, and area is clear of food debris
No structural defects that could allow pest entry (gaps, broken seals, open drains)
All dry goods are stored in sealed containers off the floor
Pest contractor's emergency contact number is available to the manager on duty
8. Equipment & Facilities
All refrigeration units are in working order and maintaining correct temperatures
Handwashing sinks are separate from food preparation sinks
Adequate ventilation and extraction in the kitchen
Lighting is adequate throughout the kitchen and storage areas
All equipment is clean and in good repair — nothing being used in a damaged state
Probe thermometers are in good working order with intact probes
