Line Cook Checklist

Role: Line Cook Updated: May 2026 Category: BOH Operations

Your station is your responsibility from the moment you arrive to the moment you hand it off. Station setup, food safety during service, and end-of-shift clean-down are all yours. Submit your checklist at the end of each shift.

1. Station Setup — Before Service

Hygiene

Wash hands before touching anything on the line
Sanitise station surfaces, boards, and immediate prep area before setting up
Confirm colour-coded boards are correct for your station — no cross-contamination
Clean uniform on, hair tied back or covered, no jewellery or nail varnish

Station Readiness

Check all station items are within date and correctly labelled
Confirm hot-hold is heating — target ≥ 145°F before service
Confirm grill, fryer, or oven is at correct service temperature
Check utensils, backup pans, towels, and liners are in place
Probe thermometer is sanitised, calibrated, and at your station

Service Brief

Read the 86 list — know what you cannot send out today
Know today's specials, pickups, and any modified allergen dishes
Know who is calling tickets and who is running food
2. During Service

Food Safety

Probe every high-risk protein before it leaves the station — poultry ≥ 165°F, fish ≥ 145°F, minced meat ≥ 165°F
Probe reheated items before placing in hot-hold — minimum 165°F throughout
Never judge doneness by colour — the probe is the only check
Sanitise probe between each protein type
Any food out of temperature — stop service and tell the manager immediately

Allergen Tickets

Every allergen-flagged ticket requires the Allergen Handling SOP — clean board, dedicated utensils, verbal pass confirmation
Never assume a dish is allergen-safe — check the recipe, check the ticket
Call out the allergen aloud when handing to the pass

Station Hygiene

Keep station clear — no build-up of debris or spills during service
Wash hands after touching raw proteins, face, phone, or leaving the kitchen
Any cut — stop, clean, cover with blue detectable plaster, rewash hands before continuing
3. End of Shift
Cover and label every item remaining on your station — name, prep date, use-by date
Any hot food remaining must be cooled correctly before refrigerating — ice bath first
Sanitise all station surfaces and boards
Return all knives to the magnetic strip
Clear and clean hot-hold equipment and empty any unused product
Note any low stock, equipment issues, or quality problems for the closer or manager
Submit Checklist

Submit at the end of each shift. Logged automatically in the manager's Google Sheet.

Submit Line Cook Checklist

Log any temperature readings or corrective actions in the Daily Temperature Log as they happen — not at the end of your shift.
