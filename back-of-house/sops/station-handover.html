BOH Shift · Back of House

Station Handover

Department / Area Back of House · BOH Shift
Prepared By Chef, Kitchen Manager
Date Reviewed May 2026
Version 1.0

Station Handover defines the required standard, sequence, and controls for this procedure.

Key Actions

  • Clear your current tickets before you start the handover.
  • Clean and sanitize the station.
  • Label and date everything in mise en place.
  • Brief the incoming cook.
  • Confirm handover with the kitchen manager.
1

Purpose

This SOP defines the required standard for station handover so the team follows the same safe, consistent process every shift.

2

Scope

Applies to All Kitchen and any team member assigned to this procedure, related checks, or manager follow-up.

3

Responsibilities

  • All assigned staff must follow this SOP exactly as written during the applicable task or shift.
  • The responsible manager or team (Chef, Kitchen Manager) is responsible for training staff, answering questions, and verifying that the procedure is followed.
  • The shift lead or manager must stop the task and start corrective action when the standard cannot be met.
4

Definitions / Key Terms

TermDefinition
SOPStandard Operating Procedure; the approved method for completing this task.
Person in ChargeThe manager or certified lead responsible for supervising the task and making food safety decisions during the shift.
Corrective ActionThe required response when a step, limit, record, or safety standard is missed or cannot be verified.
5

Materials / Equipment Needed

  • Approved tools, equipment, utensils, and supplies required for the task.
  • Required personal protective equipment, cleaning supplies, labels, logs, or checklists.
  • Working hand sink, sanitizer, thermometer, or other food safety control equipment when applicable.
6

Procedure (Step-by-Step)

Reference

A clean handover is a professional courtesy and a food safety requirement. The incoming cook should be able to take over a station without guessing at what's prepped, what's labelled, or what's running low.

Step 1 - Clear your current tickets before you start the handover

Do not begin a station handover with open tickets on the rail. Communicate with expo: "I have two tickets still running — 10 minutes before I can hand over." If the incoming cook needs to take your tickets before you're finished, walk them through each ticket verbally so there are no surprises mid-fire.

Step 2 - Clean and sanitize the station

Wipe down all surfaces, sanitize cutting boards, and remove any food debris from the station before handover. Knives go back on the magnetic strip — not on the bench. Cloths go in the laundry, not the bin. The station the incoming cook steps into should be cleaner than the station you stepped into.

Step 3 - Label and date everything in mise en place

Every container in your mise en place must have a full label before you leave — item name, date prepared, and use-by date. Post-it notes are not labels. Unlabelled containers must be labelled or discarded — the incoming cook is not responsible for identifying what you left. If items are at the end of their shelf life, flag them clearly or remove them from the station.

Step 4 - Brief the incoming cook

Stand with the incoming cook and walk through the station verbally: what is prepped and where it is, what is running low and needs a prep pull before service, any recipe changes or modifications to specials that happened during your shift, any equipment issues (don't hide them), and any allergen-modified dishes that are on the current rail or expected. The verbal brief takes two minutes and prevents three hours of confusion.

Step 5 - Confirm handover with the kitchen manager

Before you leave the section, confirm with the kitchen manager that the handover is complete. If there are open issues — low stock, equipment faults, an allergen ticket in progress — the manager needs to know before you walk out. Do not leave a station in a manager's blind spot.

7

Safety & Compliance Considerations

Corrective Action

If the standard cannot be met

Stop the task, tell the manager, isolate any affected food, equipment, or records, and follow the corrective action process before resuming.

If food safety may be affected

Do not serve or store the affected item until a manager confirms the next action. Discard when safety cannot be verified.

8

Quality Control / Verification

Follow Sequence Complete steps in order
Stop If Unsure Ask the manager before guessing
Keep Clean Reset tools and surfaces after use
9

Documentation & Records

Record any failed check, corrective action, manager decision, or required sign-off before the end of the shift.
