Pass Standards

Role: Line Cook · Expo Category: BOH SOP Updated: May 2026
The pass is the last quality check before the guest. Nothing goes through if it doesn't look right — wrong portion, wrong plating, wrong temperature, or a missing allergen call-out. Pull it back and correct it.

Step 1

Check every dish before it hits the pass

Before any plate is called to the pass, verify: correct dish for the ticket, portion weight or count as per spec, plating is clean (no smears, no finger marks, garnish in place), and the dish is at the right temperature. Wipe the rim if needed. A dish that doesn't meet spec gets corrected before it is called — not after the runner has already walked it.

Step 2

Call allergen dishes separately and out loud

When an allergen dish comes to the pass, it is called separately from the rest of the table's food — always. The call is spoken out loud regardless of whether expo is standing right there: "Allergen dish, table 5, cover 2 — no nuts, clean setup." The runner must repeat the table, cover, and allergen back before the plate leaves the pass. If the runner doesn't repeat it, the plate does not leave.

Step 3

Confirm the full table before you call the run

Do not send a partial run if all dishes for a table are ready within 30 seconds of each other. Hold and confirm all dishes are plated, checked, and at temperature before calling the runner. Sending a table in two trips fragments the guest experience and creates confusion about what has and hasn't been delivered.

Step 4

Keep the pass clear — plates move or come back

The pass is not a holding shelf. Plates sitting at the pass that haven't been called means a runner isn't ready — tell expo immediately so they can find one. If a dish has been sitting and has lost temperature, it comes back to the station for a re-fire or a heat check. A warm plate going cold at the pass is a kitchen problem, not a FOH problem.

Step 5

If it doesn't look right, pull it back

Any dish that fails the check — wrong portion, wrong garnish, wrong temperature, plating doesn't meet the recipe spec — comes back to the station. Tell the relevant cook what needs fixing, fire the correction, and call a hold to expo so FOH can manage the table. Never send a dish you wouldn't be comfortable putting in front of the chef.
