BOH Closing Checklist

Area: Back of House Updated: May 2026
DateShiftSigned by

Review what's expected at end of service, then tap the button to submit. Nothing leaves the kitchen until the form is submitted.

What to complete this close

Equipment Shutdown

Scrape and clean grill while still hot
Turn off fryer — do not leave on overnight
Season grill grates after clean and cool-down
Turn off all oven and hot-hold equipment
Confirm all pilot lights / gas valves are off

Food Storage

Label and date all prepped items going back into the walk-in
Cover all containers — no open food in the fridge
Log final fridge and freezer temperatures
Return all knives to the magnetic strip

Cleaning

Sanitise all prep surfaces and cutting boards
Clean and sanitise sinks
Sweep and mop kitchen floor
Empty and reline all bins
Remove bin bags to external waste area
Clean behind and under fryer (weekly)

Security

Lock walk-in cooler
Lock dry storage room
Set alarm and lock up
Submit Closing Checklist

Submissions are timestamped and logged automatically. If anything was left unfinished, note it in the final "flags for manager" field.
