BOH Opening Checklist

Area: Back of House Updated: May 2026

Review what's expected this shift, then tap the button below to submit today's checklist. The form is timestamped and goes straight to the manager's log.

What to expect this shift

Temperatures

Log fridge temperature (must be ≤ 41°F)
Log freezer temperature (must be ≤ 0°F)
Probe-check a mid-shelf fridge item

Morning Setup

Check and date-label all prepped items from yesterday
Sanitise all cutting boards and prep surfaces
Set up handwashing station — soap, paper towels stocked
Start fryer preheat
Pull protein for first service
Set up garnish station
Confirm pass is clean and clear

Stock & Storage

Check dry storage — nothing on the floor
Rotate any new stock (FIFO)
Check bin liners are in place

Equipment Check

Confirm all knives are on the magnetic strip
Check probe thermometer is calibrated and available
Confirm fryer has reached service temperature before service
Start Today's Checklist

Submissions are logged automatically in the manager's Google Sheet. If you can't access the form, tell your manager before service starts.
