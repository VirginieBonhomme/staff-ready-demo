Kitchen Prep Brief

Role: Head Chef · Kitchen Manager Updated: May 2026
DateShiftSigned by

Run this with the full kitchen team before every service. Every cook should hear the same information at the same time — allergen bookings, menu changes, and what's running low do not get passed cook-to-cook. They go through the brief.

Brief Agenda

Today's Service

Expected covers and any large parties or bookings that affect volume or timing
Section assignments — who is on each station, who is on expo, who is on dish
Any pacing notes from the manager — early push, held tables, VIP timing requirements

Menu

Specials — components, allergens, portion weight, pickup time, and plating
86 items and confirmed substitutions — stated clearly so every cook knows before service starts
Any recipe changes or modifications approved since the last service
Dishes with longer pickup times flagged — so expo knows to call them early

Allergen Handover

All allergy bookings read aloud — table number, dietary requirement, and confirmed modification
Allergen tickets confirmed in the system and at the pass before first ticket drops
Clean-setup protocol confirmed — which station handles allergen tickets and what equipment is dedicated
Any new allergens or ingredient changes that affect the current allergen matrix

Station Readiness

All stations confirm mise en place is complete before the brief closes
Equipment issues reported — nothing hidden, everything flagged to the manager before service
Temperatures confirmed — fridge ≤ 41°F, hot hold ≥ 140°F before first ticket fires
Handwashing stations checked — soap, paper towels, and access confirmed

Previous Shift Notes

Any complaints, service misses, or near-misses from the previous shift reviewed
Stock or prep shortfalls carried forward from opening that affect today's service
Emergency roles confirmed — who calls manager, who clears the kitchen, who stays on station in a crisis
Submit Prep Brief Log

The log captures what was communicated, any issues raised, and who was present. If you can't access the form, record the brief in the kitchen log book before service starts.
