Life safety emergency
911
Call 911
Safety

Guest allergen reaction

  1. Call 911 if breathing, swelling, collapse, or severe symptoms occur.
  2. Notify the manager and stop the table’s food service.
  3. Preserve the ticket and any remaining food for review.

Escalate: General manager and chef immediately.

Food Safety

Walk-in fridge failure

  1. Check and record the temperature immediately.
  2. Keep the door closed and move high-risk food only with manager approval.
  3. Start corrective action log and call maintenance.

Escalate: Kitchen manager before using any affected food.

Operations

Power outage

  1. Stop cooking equipment and secure open flames.
  2. Hold service until manager confirms guest and food safety.
  3. Record affected refrigeration, payments, and open checks.

Escalate: Manager, utility provider, and owner.
