Braised Short Rib

Role: Line Cook Station: Hot Line Updated: June 2026
Batch Yield 12 portions
Portion 1 rib + 4 oz potato
Pickup 8-10 min
Shelf Life 3 days

Service standard: reheat short rib to 165°F throughout before plating. Sauce must be hot, glossy, and free of broken fat. Do not send dry, gray, or underheated ribs to the pass.

Contains Milk Wheat Celery Sulphites
Build Sheet
Batch Ingredients
Short Rib Braise
12 eachBone-in beef short ribs, trimmed
1 qtRed wineUse approved house cooking wine only.
2 qtBeef stock
8 ozTomato paste
2 lbMirepoix: onion, carrot, celery
6 eachGarlic cloves, crushed
as neededKosher salt, black pepper, thyme, bay leaf
Plate Components
4 ozCreamed potato per plate
2 ozReduced braising jus per plate
1 pinchPickled mustard seed
3 sprigsWashed parsley or watercress garnish
Prep Method

Step 1

Sear and build flavor

Season ribs on all sides. Sear in a hot rondeau until deeply browned. Remove ribs. Add mirepoix and garlic, cook until lightly caramelized, then add tomato paste and cook for 2 minutes.

Quality cue: ribs should have dark brown color before braising. Pale sear creates weak sauce.

Step 2

Braise covered

Deglaze with red wine and reduce by half. Add beef stock, herbs, and ribs. Cover tightly. Braise at 300°F until fork tender, usually 3 to 3.5 hours.

Do not rush this step. If the bone does not move easily, continue cooking in 20-minute checks.

Step 3

Cool, label, and store

Cool ribs in shallow containers with braising liquid. Label with item name, prep date, discard date, and initials. Store covered in the walk-in once properly cooled.

Follow the cooling SOP. If cooling limits cannot be verified, escalate to a manager before using the batch.

Service Pickup
1 Warm one rib with 2 oz jus in a covered pan until 165°F throughout. Probe
2 Place 4 oz creamed potato slightly off center in a warm bowl. Plate
3 Set rib on potato, spoon hot jus around the base, and finish with garnish. Finish
4 Wipe rim and confirm sauce, garnish, temperature, and portion before handoff. Expo
Quality, Safety & Records
Critical temp Reheat to 165°F throughout before service. Record corrective action if product is below standard.
Hold Hold hot sauce at 140°F or above during service. Discard any sauce with unknown time or temperature history.
Allergens Contains milk, wheat, celery, and sulphites. Escalate any allergy request to the manager before firing.
Records Cooling log, reheating check, prep label, and batch discard date.
