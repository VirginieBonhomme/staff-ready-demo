Service Standards
Allergy Never guess or promise an item is safe. Confirm with the kitchen through the manager or expo before the order is placed. Non-Negotiable
Escalate If a guest has a reaction, a complaint about food safety, or you are unsure about an ingredient, get a manager immediately. Always
Checklist The opening checklist is complete when every item is checked and the form is submitted. Not when it feels done. Standard
