Harbor & Hearth Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)
DOH-PEST-07

DOH-Informed · Facility Protection

DOH Pest & Waste Control

Department / Area DOH-Informed · Facility Protection
Prepared By Manager / PIC
Date Reviewed June 2026
Version 1.0

Integrated pest management, daily pest evidence checks, waste handling, licensed pest control, and pesticide safety.

Key Actions

  • Inspect daily for pest evidence and conditions that attract pests.
  • Remove food, water, harborage, and entry points.
  • Keep refuse covered, clean, and away from food areas.
  • Use licensed pest professionals and keep contract/service records available.
1

Purpose

Prevent rodents, insects, and other pests from contaminating food, equipment, utensils, storage areas, and customer spaces.

2

Scope

Applies to DOH-Informed · Facility Protection and any staff member assigned to this process, task, equipment, checklist, or manager follow-up.

3

Responsibilities

  • All assigned staff must follow this SOP exactly as written during the applicable task or shift.
  • The responsible manager or team (Manager / PIC) is responsible for training staff, answering questions, and verifying that the procedure is followed.
  • The shift lead or manager must stop the task and start corrective action when the standard cannot be met.
4

Definitions / Key Terms

TermDefinition
SOPStandard Operating Procedure; the approved method for completing this task.
Person in ChargeThe manager or certified lead responsible for supervising the task and making food safety decisions during the shift.
Corrective ActionThe required response when a step, limit, record, or safety standard is missed or cannot be verified.
5

Materials / Equipment Needed

  • Food service establishments must be kept free of pests and conditions conducive to pests.
  • Managers must inspect the facility daily for pest presence and conditions that allow entry or harborage.
  • A pest management professional contract and service records must be maintained and available.
  • Preventive pest service must occur at least monthly where required by Article 81.
  • Openings that allow pest entry must be sealed; door gaps may require sweeps or barriers, including tight thresholds.
  • Pesticides may be applied only by properly licensed pest professionals. No spraying in food prep/service areas while food is exposed.
  • Food-contact surfaces affected by pesticide application must be protected and cleaned/sanitized before reuse.
6

Procedure (Step-by-Step)

When To Check

  • During opening walk-through before food prep begins.
  • During closing after cleaning and trash removal.
  • After receiving deliveries and breaking down cardboard.
  • Any time droppings, gnaw marks, insects, eggs, odors, grease buildup, standing water, or damaged doors/screens are observed.
  • After pest control service to verify recommendations were handled.

Step 1 - Inspect the outside first

Check garbage area, delivery door, back door, drains, standing water, weeds, grease, and gaps that could attract or admit pests.

Step 2 - Inspect food and storage areas

Look under shelving, behind equipment, near motors, around floor-wall junctions, in dry storage, and inside cardboard-heavy areas.

Step 3 - Remove attractants

Clean spills, crumbs, grease, food debris, standing water, and clutter. Keep food sealed and off the floor.

Step 4 - Control waste

Keep garbage containers covered, lined, clean, and emptied before overflow. Do not allow trash or recyclables to create harborage.

Step 5 - Escalate evidence immediately

Photograph or document evidence, isolate affected food/equipment, notify manager, and contact pest control when needed.

7

Safety & Compliance Considerations

Immediate Corrective Action

  • Discard food contaminated by pests or pest evidence.
  • Clean and sanitize affected food-contact surfaces before reuse.
  • Seal holes, repair door sweeps/screens, remove clutter, and correct standing water or grease accumulation.
  • Schedule pest professional service for active evidence or repeated activity.
  • Document the finding, location, action taken, and pest control follow-up.

Inspection Notes

  • Self-inspection items include pest activity, pesticide misuse, pest management contract failures, and records not available.
  • Inspectors check for droppings, live/dead insects, gnaw marks, entry gaps, standing water, clutter, and uncovered refuse.
  • A clean pest log with follow-through is stronger than verbal assurance.
8

Quality Control / Verification

  • Confirm each step was completed in the required order and within the required standard.
  • Manager or shift lead verifies the finished work before the station, item, or record is released for use.
  • Correct and document any missed step, unsafe condition, incomplete record, or unclear responsibility.
9

Documentation & Records

  • Daily pest sighting or facility walk notes where used.
  • Pest management contract.
  • Monthly service records and technician recommendations.
  • Corrective action log for pest evidence, sanitation failures, entry points, or waste issues.
  • Waste area cleaning schedule.
