Peaches Standard Operating Procedure (SOP)
SOP-FS-02

Food Safety · Opening Lead

Temperature Logging

Department / Area Kitchen · Refrigeration
Prepared By Chef / Kitchen Manager
Date Reviewed June 2026
Version Version 1.0

Check, verify, and record fridge, freezer, and hot-hold temperatures before service so unsafe food is caught before it reaches guests.

Owner note: this shows how food safety standards can be turned into a repeatable staff action instead of a paper log that gets filled in later.

Key Actions

  • Check the display temperature first.
  • Probe a product to verify the unit is holding safely.
  • Record the check before service starts.
  • Escalate immediately if food is out of range.
1

Purpose

This SOP defines the required standard for temperature logging so staff complete the task safely, consistently, and in the correct order.

2

Scope

Applies to Kitchen · Refrigeration and any staff member assigned to this process, task, equipment, checklist, or manager follow-up.

3

Responsibilities

  • All assigned staff must follow this SOP exactly as written during the applicable task or shift.
  • The responsible manager or team (Chef / Kitchen Manager) is responsible for training staff, answering questions, and verifying that the procedure is followed.
  • The shift lead or manager must stop the task and start corrective action when the standard cannot be met.
4

Definitions / Key Terms

TermDefinition
SOPStandard Operating Procedure; the approved method for completing this task.
Person in ChargeThe manager or certified lead responsible for supervising the task and making food safety decisions during the shift.
Corrective ActionThe required response when a step, limit, record, or safety standard is missed or cannot be verified.
5

Materials / Equipment Needed

  • Approved tools, equipment, utensils, and supplies required for the task.
  • Required personal protective equipment, cleaning supplies, labels, logs, or checklists.
  • Working hand sink, sanitizer, thermometer, or other food safety control equipment when applicable.
6

Procedure (Step-by-Step)

Step 1

Check the unit display

Read the fridge, freezer, or hot-hold display before opening doors or moving product.

Step 2

Probe a product

Probe a dense item in the middle of the unit to confirm product temperature, not just air temperature.

Step 3

Record and escalate

Log the reading immediately. If the reading is outside standard, hold product and notify the manager.

7

Safety & Compliance Considerations

Critical Limits

  • Chilled food: 41°F or below
  • Frozen food: 0°F or below
  • Hot holding: 135°F or above

Corrective Action

If food is above the safe limit, isolate it, keep the door closed, notify the manager, and document the decision before using or discarding product.

8

Quality Control / Verification

  • Confirm each step was completed in the required order and within the required standard.
  • Manager or shift lead verifies the finished work before the station, item, or record is released for use.
  • Correct and document any missed step, unsafe condition, incomplete record, or unclear responsibility.
9

Documentation & Records

  • Complete any required checklist, log, label, or manager note tied to this SOP.
  • Record corrective actions with the date, time, item or area affected, action taken, and manager initials.
  • Keep completed records in the assigned location for manager review and inspection readiness.
