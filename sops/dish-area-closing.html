Dish Area Closing

Role: Dishwasher Updated: May 2026 Category: Dishwashing

Step 1

Run all remaining dishes before breakdown

Clear the dish area of remaining plates, pans, utensils, racks, and glassware before taking the machine apart. Check the line, prep area, pass, and server station for late items so clean storage is complete before the final reset.

Step 2

Drain and clean the dish machine

Turn the machine off, drain it fully, and remove scrap trays, filters, curtains, and removable spray arms according to the machine setup. Wash all removable parts, clear food debris, and leave parts installed only after they are clean.

Step 3

Clean sinks, counters, and drainboards

Empty all sinks and remove food debris from strainers. Wash and sanitize sink basins, taps, handles, counters, drainboards, wall splash areas, and rack tracks. No standing water, loose food, or chemical puddles should remain.

Step 4

Reset racks, chemicals, and floors

Stack clean racks neatly, restock soap and sanitizer if needed, take dish-area trash out, and sweep and mop the floor under the machine, sinks, shelves, and mats. Hang mats to dry if used. Report low chemicals or machine issues before leaving.

Note — The dish area is closed only when clean storage is full, the machine is clean, and the floor is safe to walk on.
