Prep · Back of House

Cold Prep Setup

Department / Area Back of House · Prep
Prepared By Chef, Kitchen Manager
Date Reviewed May 2026
Version 1.0

Set the cold prep station in a clean, organized, food-safe order before any food is pulled, cut, labelled, or moved to service.

1

Purpose

This SOP explains how to prepare the cold prep station so food is handled on sanitized surfaces, with the correct boards, labels, stock rotation, and garnish setup.

2

Scope

Applies to prep cooks, line cooks, openers, and any team member setting up cold prep, garnish, salad, sandwich, dessert, or ready-to-eat food stations.

3

Tools Needed

  • Food-safe sanitizer spray and clean wiping cloth
  • Correct color-coded cutting boards
  • Clean knives, tongs, spoons, and prep utensils
  • Labels, marker, and date-coding guide
  • Clean containers, lids, squeeze bottles, and garnish pans
  • Probe thermometer if checking chilled items
4

Procedure

Step 1 — Sanitize the station

Spray the full prep surface with food-safe sanitizer. Wipe with a clean cloth in overlapping strokes and allow the surface to air-dry for 30 seconds before placing food, boards, or utensils on it.

Step 2 — Set boards by color

Choose the correct board for the food category before food is pulled. Do not substitute board colors. If a board is missing, stop and tell the manager or chef.

Step 3 — Check yesterday's prep first

Before pulling new stock, check every existing container for item name, prep date, use-by date, condition, and storage temperature. Move oldest usable stock to the front.

Step 4 — Label all new prep

Every container must be labelled before it goes into the walk-in, lowboy, reach-in, or service station. Include item name, prep date, use-by date, and initials where required.

Step 5 — Set garnish last

Set garnish pans after prep is complete and food is returned to cold storage. Arrange containers in menu order, fill two-thirds full, and keep the station clean before service starts.

5

Board Reference

Color
Use For
■ Red
Raw red meat
■ Yellow
Raw poultry
■ Blue
Raw fish and seafood
■ Green
Fruit, salad, and vegetables
■ White
Cooked and ready-to-eat food
6

Standards

Surface Sanitized and air-dried
Labels Name, prep date, use-by
Rotation Oldest usable stock first

Unlabelled Food

Any unlabelled container must be shown to the supervisor. Do not guess what it is, relabel it from memory, or move it into service.

7

Corrective Action

If the station was not sanitized

Stop prep, remove food and tools, sanitize the surface correctly, and replace any food that may have contacted the unsanitized surface.

If the wrong board was used

Stop prep, isolate the affected food, tell the manager, and follow the Corrective Action SOP.

If prep is out of temperature

Move the item back to cold storage or discard as directed by the manager. Log any failed temperature or corrective action required.
