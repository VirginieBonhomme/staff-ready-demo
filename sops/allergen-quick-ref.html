Allergen Quick Reference

Department / Area All Departments · Allergens
Prepared By Manager, Kitchen Manager
Date Reviewed May 2026
Version 1.0

A guest allergy is never a preference. If you are not 100% certain a dish is safe for a guest, you stop, you ask, and you get a confirmed answer before the food leaves the kitchen or the bar.

1

Purpose

This SOP defines the required standard for allergen quick reference so the team follows the same safe, consistent process every shift.

2

Scope

Applies to All Staff and any team member assigned to this procedure, related checks, or manager follow-up.

3

Reference

This matrix covers the current menu only. If a dish is modified or a new special is added, the allergen information must be confirmed with the head chef before service and updated here.

When a Guest Flags an Allergy

1
Stop and confirm the allergen

Do not proceed with the order until you know exactly which allergen is flagged. Ask the guest directly if the ticket is unclear.

2
Enter it in the POS immediately

FOH enters the allergen as a modifier on the order. The kitchen sees it on the ticket. This is not optional and is not done after the order is placed.

3
Kitchen checks every component

The chef checks the dish against this matrix. If any component is uncertain — sauces, garnishes, oils, batters — stop and escalate to the head chef before cooking.

4
Prepare on a clean surface with clean tools

Fresh board, clean gloves, clean utensils from storage. Do not use shared tools from the bain-marie. Do not plate next to a dish that contains the allergen.

5
Call it out on the pass

Say it aloud: "Table 4 — allergen dish, no milk." The server confirms before the dish leaves the pass. The guest is told which dish is theirs before it is placed.

4

Procedure

Follow the Procedure

Complete the procedure using the standards on this page. Stop and ask a manager before continuing if any step cannot be completed safely.

5

Standards

Follow Sequence Complete steps in order
Stop If Unsure Ask the manager before guessing
Keep Clean Reset tools and surfaces after use
6

Corrective Action

If the standard cannot be met

Stop the task, tell the manager, isolate any affected food, equipment, or records, and follow the corrective action process before resuming.

If food safety may be affected

Do not serve or store the affected item until a manager confirms the next action. Discard when safety cannot be verified.

7

Records

Record any failed check, corrective action, manager decision, or required sign-off before the end of the shift.
