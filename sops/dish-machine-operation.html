Dishwashing · Back of House

Dish Machine Operation

Department / Area Back of House · Dishwashing
Prepared By Chef, Kitchen Manager
Date Reviewed May 2026
Version 1.0

Dish Machine Operation defines the required standard, sequence, and controls for this procedure.

1

Purpose

This SOP defines the required standard for dish machine operation so the team follows the same safe, consistent process every shift.

2

Scope

Applies to Dishwasher and any team member assigned to this procedure, related checks, or manager follow-up.

3

Procedure

Step 1 - Check the machine before the first rack

Confirm the scrap trays, filters, and spray arms are seated correctly before turning the machine on. Make sure detergent and sanitizer containers are connected and not empty. If a line is loose or a container is empty, tell the manager before running dishes.

Step 2 - Scrape, sort, and pre-rinse

Scrape food into the waste bin before anything enters the dish area sink. Sort glassware, plates, flatware, utensils, and pans into separate racks or batches. Pre-rinse heavy soil so the machine can sanitize instead of carrying food debris through the cycle.

Note — Never put knives loose into a sink or rack. Place them handle-out in the marked knife container.

Step 3 - Load racks without nesting items

Place items so water can reach every surface. Plates face the same direction, cups and containers sit upside down, and utensils are spread out instead of packed together. Do not stack pans, bowls, or deli containers inside each other.

Step 4 - Air dry before putting away

Let clean items air dry fully on the clean side before stacking or storing. Do not towel dry clean dishes, pans, or utensils. If an item still has food, grease, lipstick, or film after the cycle, send it through again.

Note — Clean items never go back onto the dirty side of the station.

4

Standards

Follow Sequence Complete steps in order
Stop If Unsure Ask the manager before guessing
Keep Clean Reset tools and surfaces after use
5

Corrective Action

If the standard cannot be met

Stop the task, tell the manager, isolate any affected food, equipment, or records, and follow the corrective action process before resuming.

If food safety may be affected

Do not serve or store the affected item until a manager confirms the next action. Discard when safety cannot be verified.

6

Records

Record any failed check, corrective action, manager decision, or required sign-off before the end of the shift.
