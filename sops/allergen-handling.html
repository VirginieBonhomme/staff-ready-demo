Allergens · Back of House

Allergen Handling

Department / Area Back of House · Allergens
Prepared By Chef, Kitchen Manager
Date Reviewed May 2026
Version 1.0

Treat every allergy ticket as a critical food safety control. Read the ticket, confirm the allergen, create a clean setup, and pass the dish with a clear verbal confirmation.

Key Actions

  • Read and confirm the allergen ticket before starting.
  • Build a clean setup before handling food.
  • Plate separately and label clearly.
  • Call allergen status at handoff and delivery.
1

Purpose

This SOP explains how kitchen staff receive, prepare, plate, and hand off allergen-modified dishes without cross-contact.

2

Scope

Applies to all kitchen staff, expo, runners, managers, and any team member involved in preparing or delivering a dish flagged with an allergy or serious dietary restriction.

3

Responsibilities

  • All assigned staff must follow this SOP exactly as written during the applicable task or shift.
  • The responsible manager or team (Chef, Kitchen Manager) is responsible for training staff, answering questions, and verifying that the procedure is followed.
  • The shift lead or manager must stop the task and start corrective action when the standard cannot be met.
4

Definitions / Key Terms

TermDefinition
SOPStandard Operating Procedure; the approved method for completing this task.
Person in ChargeThe manager or certified lead responsible for supervising the task and making food safety decisions during the shift.
Corrective ActionThe required response when a step, limit, record, or safety standard is missed or cannot be verified.
5

Materials / Equipment Needed

  • Clean cutting board reserved for the allergen dish
  • Clean utensils taken directly from clean storage
  • Fresh gloves after handwashing
  • Clean plate that has not contacted other food
  • Allergen marker, flag, or ticket notation used by the restaurant
6

Procedure (Step-by-Step)

Major Allergens

Milk Eggs Fish Shellfish Tree Nuts Peanuts Wheat Soybeans Sesame

Critical Rule

An allergy is not a preference. If you are unsure about an ingredient, garnish, sauce, fryer, utensil, or prep surface, stop and ask the manager or chef before preparing the dish.

Step 1

Receive the allergen ticket and read it fully

Stop and read the full ticket before starting the dish. Confirm the guest, table, allergen, dish, and every component that may contain or have contacted the allergen.

Step 2

Build a clean setup

Wash hands, change gloves, sanitize the work area, and use a clean board and clean tools. Do not use utensils from shared containers, bains-marie, fryer baskets, or service stations unless they have been washed and sanitized first.

Step 3

Plate separately and label clearly

Plate the allergen-modified dish on a clean plate away from dishes containing the allergen. Add the restaurant's allergen marker and keep the ticket with the dish until pass confirmation.

Step 4

Call the allergen at the pass

Call the table, cover, dish, and allergen out loud. The runner or FOH lead must repeat it back before the plate leaves the pass.

7

Safety & Compliance Considerations

Corrective Action

If the dish may have contacted the allergen

Stop the dish, discard it, tell the manager or chef, and restart with a clean setup. Do not remove the allergen from the plate and send the dish.

If the allergen information is unclear

Hold the ticket and ask the manager or chef to confirm. FOH must update the guest before the dish is fired if the request cannot be safely met.

If the wrong dish leaves the pass

Notify the manager immediately, stop service to that table if needed, and follow the crisis or incident process. Record what happened and what corrective action was taken.

8

Quality Control / Verification

Confirm Ticket, allergen, dish, guest
Separate Clean setup, clean tools, clean plate
Call Back Verbal pass confirmation required

Never Guess

Do not guess ingredients, fryer use, garnish contents, sauce bases, or cross-contact risk. If the answer is not confirmed, the dish is not safe to send.

9

Documentation & Records

  • Complete any required checklist, log, label, or manager note tied to this SOP.
  • Record corrective actions with the date, time, item or area affected, action taken, and manager initials.
  • Keep completed records in the assigned location for manager review and inspection readiness.
