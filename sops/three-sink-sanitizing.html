Three-Sink Sanitizing

Role: Dishwasher Updated: May 2026 Category: Dishwashing

Step 1

Set up wash, rinse, and sanitize sinks

Fill the first sink with hot water and detergent, the second sink with clean rinse water, and the third sink with sanitizer mixed to the label direction. Keep the sinks clearly separated so dirty water does not enter the sanitizer sink.

Step 2

Wash until all soil is removed

Scrub the item fully in the wash sink, including handles, rims, corners, and undersides. Change wash water when it becomes greasy, cloudy, or cool. Sanitizer will not work properly on food residue or heavy grease.

Step 3

Rinse with clean water

Move the item to the rinse sink and remove all soap. Do not skip this step. Detergent left on a surface can weaken sanitizer and leave residue on equipment that touches food.

Step 4

Sanitize for the full contact time

Submerge the item fully in sanitizer for the contact time listed on the chemical label. Use test strips at the start of setup and whenever sanitizer is changed. If the strip does not read in the approved range, remake the sanitizer before continuing.

Note — Do not rinse sanitizer off after the contact time. Let the item air dry.
