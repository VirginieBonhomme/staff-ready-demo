Knife Safety

Role: All Kitchen Staff Updated: May 2026 Category: Equipment

Step 1

Store knives on the magnetic strip — never loose

All knives must be stored blade-inward on the designated magnetic strip above the prep station. A knife left flat on a cutting board or in a sink is a serious injury risk to you and every person reaching near it. After washing, dry immediately and return to the strip.

Note — Never place a knife in a sink of soapy water. It becomes invisible and a serious cut risk.

Step 2

Carry a knife point-down, blade inward

When walking with a knife, hold it at your side with the point facing the floor and blade edge turned away from your body. Announce yourself when passing behind someone — say "sharp" clearly and wait for acknowledgment before moving through a tight space.

Note — "Sharp behind" is the call. Use it every time, even if the kitchen seems empty.

Step 3

Pass a knife on the board, never hand-to-hand

To pass a knife to another person, set it flat on the cutting board in front of them and let them pick it up. Direct hand-to-hand transfer is never permitted. If someone reaches for a knife you're holding, stop and place it on the nearest surface first.

Step 4

Use a stable board — damp cloth underneath

Place a damp cloth or paper towel under your cutting board before beginning any knife work. A sliding board is one of the most common causes of knife injuries. Check that the board is flat and secure every time you begin a new task, not just at the start of a shift.

Note — Report any cracked or deeply scored boards to your supervisor. They harbour bacteria and reduce grip stability.
