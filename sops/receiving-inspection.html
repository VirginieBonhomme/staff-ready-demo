Food Safety · Back of House

Receiving & Inspection

Department / Area Back of House · Food Safety
Prepared By Chef, Kitchen Manager
Date Reviewed May 2026
Version 1.0

Never sign a delivery note before inspecting the goods. Once you sign, you accept responsibility for the condition of everything in that delivery. Inspect first, then sign — or reject what fails.

Key Actions

  • Check the delivery vehicle and driver.
  • Inspect packaging for damage, labelling, and use-by dates.
  • Probe-check temperatures of chilled and frozen goods.
  • Reject goods that do not meet receiving standards immediately.
  • Store immediately and rotate FIFO.
1

Purpose

This SOP defines the required standard for receiving & inspection so the team follows the same safe, consistent process every shift.

2

Scope

Applies to Receiver / Manager and any team member assigned to this procedure, related checks, or manager follow-up.

3

Responsibilities

  • All assigned staff must follow this SOP exactly as written during the applicable task or shift.
  • The responsible manager or team (Chef, Kitchen Manager) is responsible for training staff, answering questions, and verifying that the procedure is followed.
  • The shift lead or manager must stop the task and start corrective action when the standard cannot be met.
4

Definitions / Key Terms

TermDefinition
SOPStandard Operating Procedure; the approved method for completing this task.
Person in ChargeThe manager or certified lead responsible for supervising the task and making food safety decisions during the shift.
Corrective ActionThe required response when a step, limit, record, or safety standard is missed or cannot be verified.
5

Materials / Equipment Needed

  • Approved tools, equipment, utensils, and supplies required for the task.
  • Required personal protective equipment, cleaning supplies, labels, logs, or checklists.
  • Working hand sink, sanitizer, thermometer, or other food safety control equipment when applicable.
6

Procedure (Step-by-Step)

Step 1 - Check the delivery vehicle and driver

Before unloading, check that the delivery vehicle is clean, refrigerated units are running, and there is no evidence of pests or contamination inside the vehicle. The driver should be able to confirm the temperature readings for chilled and frozen goods during transit. If the vehicle is clearly unclean or temperature-compromised, reject the delivery and contact your manager immediately.

Step 2 - Inspect packaging for damage, labelling, and use-by dates

Before moving product into storage, inspect every case or box. Look for: torn or wet packaging, evidence of freezer burn or thawing, damaged seals on vacuum-packed items, missing or illegible use-by dates, and any signs of pest activity. Reject items with missing date labels — you have no way to determine safe use-by.

Note — Check allergen labelling on any new or substitute product from the supplier. If the product has changed and allergen information differs from what you expected, notify the head chef before storing or using it.

Step 3 - Probe-check temperatures of chilled and frozen goods

Take a probe temperature of at least one item from each chilled category received. Insert the probe between sealed packages — do not pierce packaging to probe. Chilled goods must arrive at or below 46°F (ideally 41°F or below). Frozen goods must arrive solidly frozen — any partial thaw or significant ice crystals may indicate temperature abuse in transit.

Note — Record the probe temperature for each category on the Receiving Log. If a chilled item arrives above 46°F, reject it and log the rejection.

Step 4 - Reject goods that do not meet receiving standards immediately

Any item that fails on temperature, packaging, labelling, date, or quality must be set aside and clearly marked "REJECTED." Do not mix rejected items with accepted stock. Contact the supplier to arrange collection or credit. Document all rejections on the Receiving Log with the reason for rejection and the action taken.

Reject criteria summary:

  • Chilled goods above 46°F on arrival
  • Frozen goods showing signs of thaw
  • Missing or expired use-by date
  • Damaged, torn, or compromised packaging
  • Evidence of pest activity (droppings, damage, odour)
  • Unusual color, texture, or odour in fresh produce or protein

Step 5 - Store immediately and rotate FIFO

Accepted items must be moved into correct storage immediately — do not leave chilled goods sitting in the receiving area. Rotate stock using FIFO: new stock goes behind or below existing stock so older product is used first. Apply date labels to any unlabelled prep items before storing. See the Dry Storage SOP and Date Labelling SOP for storage order and labelling rules.

Note — Raw proteins must always be stored below ready-to-eat foods in the fridge. See the Color Coding SOP for the correct fridge storage hierarchy.

Step 6 - Sign the delivery note and file the Receiving Log

Once all items have been inspected and accepted stock is stored, sign the delivery note. Keep a copy for your records alongside the completed Receiving Log for that delivery. The signed Receiving Log must be retained for a minimum of 3 months and be available for inspection by environmental health officers on request.

Note — Log every delivery, including those with no issues. Consistent records demonstrate due diligence to inspectors and help track supplier quality over time.

7

Safety & Compliance Considerations

Corrective Action

If the standard cannot be met

Stop the task, tell the manager, isolate any affected food, equipment, or records, and follow the corrective action process before resuming.

If food safety may be affected

Do not serve or store the affected item until a manager confirms the next action. Discard when safety cannot be verified.

8

Quality Control / Verification

Follow Sequence Complete steps in order
Stop If Unsure Ask the manager before guessing
Keep Clean Reset tools and surfaces after use
9

Documentation & Records

Record any failed check, corrective action, manager decision, or required sign-off before the end of the shift.
