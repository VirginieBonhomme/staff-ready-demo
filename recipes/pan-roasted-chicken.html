Pan-Roasted Chicken

Category: Mains Station: Grill / Oven Updated: May 2026
Yield10 portions
Portion1 supreme
Pickup12 min
Cook temp165°F

Minimum safe core temperature for poultry: 165°F throughout. Probe every portion at the thickest part before it leaves the kitchen. Colour is not a food safety indicator — always use a calibrated thermometer.

ContainsMilkSulphites
Ingredients
Chicken and pickup
10Chicken supremes, skin on, wing bone trimmed
60 mlNeutral oil
80 gUnsalted butter, diced for basting
10 sprigsThyme
to seasonFine salt and cracked black pepper
Carrot puree and greens
1.5 kgCarrots, peeled and sliced evenly
500 mlChicken stock, hot
120 gUnsalted butter
1.2 kgSeasonal greens, washed and picked
300 mlWhite wine thyme jus, hot for service

Step 1

Trim, season, and dry the chicken skin

Trim any loose skin, sinew, or bone fragments from the supremes. Pat dry with paper towel, season lightly on both sides, and place skin-side up on a rack over a tray. Hold uncovered in the fridge for at least 2 hours before service so the skin dries and crisps properly.

Do not stack chicken. Stacked skin steams and will not crisp in the pan.

Step 2

Make and pass the carrot puree

Cook carrots in salted water until completely tender. Drain well, then blend with hot chicken stock just until moving. Add butter a little at a time until glossy. Pass through a fine sieve, season with salt, and hold hot for service or cool rapidly if prepping ahead.

Step 3

Blanch greens and set the station

Blanch greens in heavily salted boiling water until bright and just tender. Refresh in iced water, drain fully, and store on paper towel in a labelled container. Before service, set chicken, puree, greens, butter, thyme, probe, and hot jus within reach.

Step 4

Sear skin-side down, roast, and rest

Heat oil in a heavy pan until shimmering. Place chicken skin-side down and press gently for 10 seconds so the skin stays flat. Cook until evenly golden, turn, add butter and thyme, baste once, then roast at 375°F until the thickest part reaches 165°F. Rest for 3 minutes before plating.

Probe every portion. Colour is not a food safety check.

Plating

Puree base, greens tucked, chicken skin-up

Spoon carrot puree in a smooth oval just off-centre. Place hot greens alongside the puree. Set the chicken skin-up with the thick end facing the guest. Spoon 40 ml hot thyme jus around the chicken, not over the skin, and wipe the rim before sending.

Storage & Shelf Life
ChickenHold raw below 41°F, covered once dry. Use within 2 days of delivery.
PureeCool rapidly, label, and use within 3 days. Reheat to piping hot before service.
GreensUse same day after blanching. Discard if waterlogged or discoloured.
