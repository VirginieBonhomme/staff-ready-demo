Lemon Posset

Category: Dessert Station: Pastry Updated: May 2026
Yield12 portions
Portion110 g
Set time4 hrs
Pickup2 min
ContainsMilkGluten
Ingredients
Posset
1.2 LDouble cream
300 gCaster sugar
180 mlFresh lemon juice, strained
2 lemonsZest, microplaned
To finish
360 gBerry compote
180 gShortbread crumb
12Mint tips or small lemon balm leaves

Step 1

Boil cream, sugar, and zest

Combine cream, sugar, and lemon zest in a heavy pan. Bring to a controlled boil, stirring constantly around the base and corners so the cream does not catch. Once boiling, cook for 3 minutes to dissolve sugar fully and build the right set.

Step 2

Add lemon juice, pass, and portion

Remove the pan from the heat and whisk in strained lemon juice. The mix will thicken slightly. Pass through a fine sieve into a jug, then pour 110 g into each clean pot. Work while warm so the portions are even.

If lemon seeds or zest pieces remain, the batch was not passed correctly.

Step 3

Chill uncovered, then cover once cold

Place pots on a level tray and chill uncovered until cold. Once cold, cover and label. Do not cover while hot or condensation will drip onto the surface and mark the set.

Plating

Compote, crumb, garnish, clean rim

Spoon 30 g berry compote onto each set posset. Add 15 g shortbread crumb at the last moment so it stays crisp. Finish with one mint tip or lemon balm leaf. Wipe the pot rim before it leaves the pass.

If the posset is loose after 4 hours, do not serve it. Flag the batch before service.

Storage & Shelf Life
PossetCover once cold. Use within 3 days. Keep below 41°F.
CompoteKeep chilled and labelled. Use within 5 days or discard if fermented.
CrumbStore airtight at room temperature. Keep dry and discard if soft.
