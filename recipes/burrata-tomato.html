Burrata & Tomato

Category: Starter Station: Cold Updated: May 2026
Yield8 portions
Portion1 burrata
Pickup3 min
StationCold
ContainsMilkGlutenSulphites
Ingredients
Per 8 portions
8Burrata, drained but kept moist
1.2 kgMixed tomatoes, ripe, cut unevenly for texture
120 mlBasil oil
80 mlSherry vinaigrette
8 slicesSourdough, sliced for toast
to finishMaldon salt, basil leaves, cracked black pepper

Step 1

Cut and season tomatoes close to service

Cut tomatoes no more than 2 hours before service. Use a mix of wedges, halves, and thick slices so the plate has height and texture. Season with salt and sherry vinaigrette, then stand for 15 minutes. Drain excess liquid before plating.

Step 2

Temper burrata and set cold station

Remove burrata from the fridge 15 minutes before service and keep covered. Set tomatoes, basil oil, vinaigrette, basil leaves, Maldon, black pepper, and sourdough on the cold station. Keep spare burrata in the fridge until needed.

Burrata should be cool, not fridge-hard. Do not leave dairy at room temperature longer than needed for service.

Step 3

Toast sourdough to order

Brush sourdough lightly with oil and toast until crisp at the edges but still easy to bite. Season with a little salt. Do not plate soggy or cold toast; it should be the final component before the dish leaves.

Plating

Tomatoes first, burrata off-centre, oil around

Place drained tomatoes in a low pile. Set burrata slightly off-centre and tear the top gently so the centre is visible. Spoon basil oil around the plate, finish with basil leaves, Maldon, and black pepper. Serve toast on the side.

Storage & Shelf Life
BurrataKeep in original liquid below 41°F. Use within 24 hours of opening.
TomatoesCut tomatoes are same-day only. Do not hold dressed tomatoes overnight.
Basil oilKeep chilled and labelled. Use within 3 days.
