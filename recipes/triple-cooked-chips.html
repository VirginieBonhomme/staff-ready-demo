Triple-Cooked Chips

Category: Side Station: Fryer Updated: May 2026
Yield12 portions
Portion180 g
Final fry355°F
Pickup4 min
ContainsNo declared allergens
Ingredients
Batch
4 kgMaris Piper potatoes, peeled
to fryNeutral frying oil, clean and filtered
to seasonFine salt
optionalChopped parsley or rosemary salt for specials

Step 1

Cut evenly and rinse starch away

Cut potatoes into thick chips, roughly 18 mm wide. Keep the size consistent so they cook at the same rate. Rinse in cold running water until the water runs mostly clear, then hold submerged until ready to simmer.

Step 2

Simmer until edges crack

Transfer chips to cold salted water and bring gently to a simmer. Cook until the outside edges start to crack and roughen but the chips still hold together. Lift carefully with a spider or tray, not by tipping hard into a colander.

The cracked edges become the crisp shell. If the chips are still smooth, they were not cooked far enough.

Step 3

Steam dry, blanch, and chill

Spread chips on trays and steam dry fully. Blanch in oil at 285°F until pale and set, then drain on racks. Chill uncovered in the fridge so surface moisture evaporates. Do not cover hot chips; condensation ruins the texture.

Pickup

Final fry hot, drain well, season immediately

Fry one portion at a time at 355°F until deep golden, crisp, and light when shaken. Drain for 10 seconds, tip into a warm bowl, season immediately, and serve hot. Never leave finished chips under a heat lamp for more than 3 minutes.

Do not overload fryer baskets. Crowding drops oil temperature and gives greasy chips.

Storage & Shelf Life
Blanched chipsHold uncovered in the fridge for same-day service. Discard at end of day.
OilFilter daily. If oil smells burnt or foams heavily, flag to manager before service.
