Seared Sea Bass

Category: Mains Station: Fish Updated: May 2026
Yield10 portions
Portion1 fillet
Pickup8 min
StationFish

Minimum safe core temperature: 145°F. Probe the thickest part of every fillet before it leaves the kitchen. Color and texture are not food safety checks — the probe is the only verification.

ContainsFishMilkSulphites
Ingredients
Fish and garnish
10Sea bass fillets, scaled and pin-boned
1.5 kgNew potatoes, cooked in salted water
600 gFennel, shaved thin and held iced
400 mlLemon beurre blanc
1 bunchDill, picked and held dry
to seasonFine salt, lemon juice, olive oil

Step 1

Check, trim, and dry the fish

Run fingers over every fillet and remove pin bones with fish tweezers. Trim ragged edges, score the skin with three shallow cuts, then dry the skin thoroughly. Hold skin-side up on blue paper in the fish tray.

Wet skin sticks and tears. Re-dry the fillet immediately before it goes into the pan.

Step 2

Prepare potatoes, fennel, and sauce

Cook potatoes until just tender, then crush lightly while warm with olive oil and salt. Shave fennel thinly and hold in iced water for crispness; drain fully before service. Keep beurre blanc warm at the edge of the stove, never boiling.

Step 3

Sear skin-side down until crisp

Season the fish just before cooking. Heat a non-stick pan with a thin film of oil. Lay the fish skin-side down away from you and press gently for 20 seconds. Cook mostly on the skin side until the flesh is opaque two-thirds of the way up, then turn for 30 seconds only.

Probe the thickest part before plating — minimum 145°F core. Sea bass is thin, so this is reached quickly; it is still mandatory to verify. Do not cover the pan — steam softens the skin.

Plating

Potatoes down, fish skin-up, sauce around

Warm crushed potatoes and place in the centre of the plate. Dress fennel with lemon juice and olive oil, then tuck beside the potatoes. Place fish skin-up across the potatoes. Spoon beurre blanc around the plate, finish with dill, and wipe the rim.

Storage & Shelf Life
FishUse day of delivery where possible. Hold below 41°F and discard if odour is strong or flesh is tacky.
SauceHold warm, not boiling. Split sauce must be remade before service.
FennelDrain before service and use same day once shaved.
