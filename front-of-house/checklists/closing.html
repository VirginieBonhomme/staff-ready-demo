FOH Closing Checklist

Role: Closing Server / Manager Updated: May 2026
DateShiftSigned by

The close is complete only when the room is reset, money is reconciled, service notes are handed over, and tomorrow's team can open without guessing.

FOH Closing Standard

Guest Area

All tables cleared, wiped, reset, and chairs aligned
Menus wiped and stored; damaged menus pulled for replacement
Floors swept, mats lifted if needed, spills cleaned, and lost property logged
Restrooms cleaned, stocked, bins emptied, and final check logged

Service Stations

Glassware polished and restocked for opening pars
Water bottles, cutlery, napkins, trays, and condiments reset
Bar garnish discarded or labelled correctly; ice wells handled to house standard

Admin & Cash-Up

All tables closed, tabs checked, discounts approved, and comps noted
Cash drawer counted by two people and variance recorded
Card batch checked, receipts filed, and tips recorded
Handover notes written: complaints, VIPs, maintenance, stock, and staffing issues
