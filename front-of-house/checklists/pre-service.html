Pre-Service Brief

Role: Manager / Shift Lead Updated: May 2026

Run this before every service. Keep it short, specific, and spoken out loud so the whole team hears the same information.

Brief Agenda

Today's Service

Expected covers, large parties, VIPs, celebrations, and time pressure points
Section assignments, host ownership, drink runner, food runner, and close-down owner
Table turns and pacing rules for peak booking windows

Food & Drink

Specials tasted or described with price, garnish, and key allergens
86 items, low-stock items, substitutions, and dishes with longer pickup times
Wine, cocktail, or non-alcoholic feature to recommend first

Risks

All bookings with allergies read aloud and confirmed in POS
Guest complaints or service misses from previous shift reviewed
Emergency roles confirmed: who calls manager, who clears guests, who stays at host stand
