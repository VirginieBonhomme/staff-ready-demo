FOH Opening Checklist

Role: Host / Server Lead Updated: May 2026

Complete before doors open. The goal is simple: the room is guest-ready, the team is briefed, and no guest discovers a problem before we do.

FOH Opening Standard

Room & Entrance

Unlock and inspect guest entrance, host stand, coat area, and waiting area
Check lights, music level, temperature, candles, plants, and visible dust
Walk every table and chair; fix wobbles, crumbs, stains, and uneven settings
Restrooms checked, stocked, clean, dry, and logged before service

Service Setup

Menus clean, current, and enough for expected covers
POS terminals, card readers, receipt paper, and printer tested
Water bottles, glassware, cutlery, napkins, trays, and pens stocked
Bar garnish, ice, wine keys, polishing cloths, and service stations ready

Bookings & Brief

Review reservations, VIPs, allergies, birthdays, large parties, and accessibility needs
Confirm table plan and pacing with manager
Read kitchen 86 list, specials, allergens, and timing notes
Assign sections and confirm who owns host, phones, drinks, and running food
