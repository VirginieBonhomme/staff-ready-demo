Payment & Cash-Up

Role: Server / Manager Category: FOH SOP Updated: May 2026

Step 1

Check the bill before presenting

Confirm table number, covers, courses, drinks, voids, discounts, service charge, and comps before the guest sees the bill. If there is any uncertainty, check with the manager before printing.

Step 2

Handle payment at the table professionally

Keep card terminals in view of the guest. For split payments, confirm the split method before charging. For cash, count away from the table discreetly and return change promptly with the receipt.

Step 3

Close tables immediately after payment

Close the table in POS as soon as payment is complete. Do not leave paid tables open. File signed receipts and note any unusual discounts, comps, or guest issues.

Step 4

Cash up with two-person verification

At close, count the drawer, compare cash, card totals, gift cards, tips, refunds, and discounts. Variances must be written down and signed by the manager. Cash is never left unattended on the bar or host stand.
