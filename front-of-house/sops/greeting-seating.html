Greeting & Seating

Role: Host Category: FOH SOP Updated: May 2026

Step 1

Acknowledge guests immediately

Look up and acknowledge every guest within 5 seconds of arrival, even if you are finishing another task. Use a calm greeting: “Good evening, welcome in. Do you have a reservation?” Do not leave guests standing without eye contact.

Step 2

Confirm booking details and needs

Confirm name, party size, reservation time, allergies, accessibility needs, high chair needs, celebration notes, and whether the party is complete. If there is an allergy, repeat it back and confirm it is noted before seating.

Never treat allergy notes as casual preferences. The server and kitchen must both receive the information.

Step 3

Seat to the table plan, not convenience

Seat according to the current table plan and pacing. Do not double-seat a server without manager approval. Walk guests to the table at guest pace, place menus cleanly, and avoid pointing across the room.

Step 4

Hand off clearly to the server

Tell the server table number, party size, allergy notes, celebration notes, and timing pressure. Mark the table seated in the booking system immediately. If the server is not visible, tell the floor lead.
