FOH Host · Front of House

Reservations & Waitlist

Department / Area Front of House · FOH Host
Prepared By FOH Manager, General Manager
Date Reviewed May 2026
Version 1.0

Reservations & Waitlist defines the required standard, sequence, and controls for this procedure.

1

Purpose

This SOP defines the required standard for reservations & waitlist so the team follows the same safe, consistent process every shift.

2

Scope

Applies to Host / Manager and any team member assigned to this procedure, related checks, or manager follow-up.

3

Procedure

Step 1 - Record complete booking notes

Every booking must include name, phone, party size, time, date, special occasions, allergies, accessibility needs, and seating requests. Repeat the booking back before ending the call or confirming online changes.

Step 2 - Quote wait times honestly

Quote a range, not a promise. Use the table plan, kitchen pacing, and current dessert/payment stage to estimate. If the wait changes by more than 10 minutes, update the guest before they ask.

Step 3 - Manage table turns without rushing guests

Watch course stage, bill stage, and reservation times. Alert the manager early if a table will not turn on time. Do not pressure guests directly unless the manager instructs you how to phrase it.

Step 4 - Communicate handoffs

When seating from a waitlist, tell the server if the guest waited a long time, has a deadline, has allergies, or is celebrating. Mark the table seated and remove the guest from the waitlist immediately.

4

Standards

Follow Sequence Complete steps in order
Stop If Unsure Ask the manager before guessing
Keep Clean Reset tools and surfaces after use
5

Corrective Action

If the standard cannot be met

Stop the task, tell the manager, isolate any affected food, equipment, or records, and follow the corrective action process before resuming.

If food safety may be affected

Do not serve or store the affected item until a manager confirms the next action. Discard when safety cannot be verified.

6

Records

Record any failed check, corrective action, manager decision, or required sign-off before the end of the shift.
