Allergen Communication

Role: All FOH Category: Critical Updated: May 2026

Step 1

Ask every table before ordering

Ask clearly: “Does anyone have any allergies or dietary restrictions we need to know about?” Pause and listen. Do not rush past the answer. If a guest says “a little” or “not serious,” still treat it as an allergy until a manager confirms otherwise.

Step 2

Confirm with kitchen before promising

If a guest asks whether something is safe, say: “Let me confirm that with the kitchen.” Do not guess from memory. Check the recipe, check prep exposure, and confirm with the chef or manager before answering.

The dangerous answer is “it should be fine.” Use confirmed facts only.

Step 3

Enter the allergen in the POS

Add the allergen as a clear modifier on every relevant course. Use plain language: “ALLERGY: GLUTEN” or “ALLERGY: NUTS.” Do not bury allergy notes in vague comments. Tell the expo or chef verbally as well.

Step 4

Announce allergen dishes at delivery

When the dish is delivered, say the dish name and allergen modification out loud: “Sea bass, gluten allergy confirmed.” Do not auction allergen food. Place it directly in front of the correct guest.
