Allergen Communication

Department / Area Front of House · FOH Critical
Prepared By FOH Manager, General Manager
Date Reviewed May 2026
Version 1.0

Allergen Communication defines the required standard, sequence, and controls for this procedure.

Key Actions

  • Ask every table before ordering.
  • Confirm with kitchen before promising.
  • Enter the allergen in the POS.
  • Announce allergen dishes at delivery.
1

Purpose

This SOP defines the required standard for allergen communication so the team follows the same safe, consistent process every shift.

2

Scope

Applies to FOH Staff and any team member assigned to this procedure, related checks, or manager follow-up.

3

Responsibilities

  • All assigned staff must follow this SOP exactly as written during the applicable task or shift.
  • The responsible manager or team (FOH Manager, General Manager) is responsible for training staff, answering questions, and verifying that the procedure is followed.
  • The shift lead or manager must stop the task and start corrective action when the standard cannot be met.
4

Definitions / Key Terms

TermDefinition
SOPStandard Operating Procedure; the approved method for completing this task.
Person in ChargeThe manager or certified lead responsible for supervising the task and making food safety decisions during the shift.
Corrective ActionThe required response when a step, limit, record, or safety standard is missed or cannot be verified.
5

Materials / Equipment Needed

  • Approved tools, equipment, utensils, and supplies required for the task.
  • Required personal protective equipment, cleaning supplies, labels, logs, or checklists.
  • Working hand sink, sanitizer, thermometer, or other food safety control equipment when applicable.
6

Procedure (Step-by-Step)

Step 1 - Ask every table before ordering

Ask clearly: “Does anyone have any allergies or dietary restrictions we need to know about?” Pause and listen. Do not rush past the answer. If a guest says “a little” or “not serious,” still treat it as an allergy until a manager confirms otherwise.

Step 2 - Confirm with kitchen before promising

If a guest asks whether something is safe, say: “Let me confirm that with the kitchen.” Do not guess from memory. Check the recipe, check prep exposure, and confirm with the chef or manager before answering.

The dangerous answer is “it should be fine.” Use confirmed facts only.

Step 3 - Enter the allergen in the POS

Add the allergen as a clear modifier on every relevant course. Use plain language: “ALLERGY: GLUTEN” or “ALLERGY: NUTS.” Do not bury allergy notes in vague comments. Tell the expo or chef verbally as well.

Step 4 - Announce allergen dishes at delivery

When the dish is delivered, say the dish name and allergen modification out loud: “Sea bass, gluten allergy confirmed.” Do not auction allergen food. Place it directly in front of the correct guest.

7

Safety & Compliance Considerations

Corrective Action

If the standard cannot be met

Stop the task, tell the manager, isolate any affected food, equipment, or records, and follow the corrective action process before resuming.

If food safety may be affected

Do not serve or store the affected item until a manager confirms the next action. Discard when safety cannot be verified.

8

Quality Control / Verification

Follow Sequence Complete steps in order
Stop If Unsure Ask the manager before guessing
Keep Clean Reset tools and surfaces after use
9

Documentation & Records

Record any failed check, corrective action, manager decision, or required sign-off before the end of the shift.
