Table Service Sequence

Role: Server Category: FOH SOP Updated: May 2026

Step 1

Approach within 2 minutes

Greet the table within 2 minutes of seating. Introduce yourself, offer water, explain specials briefly, and ask about allergies before taking any order. If you are delayed, ask another server or the floor lead to greet them.

Step 2

Take drinks first and set expectations

Take drinks promptly, repeat the order, and enter it before taking another table’s food order. If the bar is backed up, tell the guest early rather than waiting until they ask.

Step 3

Take food orders with modifiers confirmed

Ask for cooking temperatures where needed, confirm sides, note allergies in the POS, and repeat anything modified. Do not promise substitutions without kitchen approval. Fire courses according to the manager’s pacing standard.

Step 4

Check back after first bites

Check back within 2 minutes or 2 bites after food lands. Ask a specific question: “Is the chicken cooked how you like it?” Fix problems immediately and involve the manager when food, allergies, timing, or guest tone requires it.

Step 5

Clear, offer next step, and close cleanly

Clear only when everyone who is still eating is comfortable. Offer dessert, coffee, another drink, or the bill. Before payment, check the bill for correct table, discounts, comps, and split requests.
