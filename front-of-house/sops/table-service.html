FOH Service · Front of House

Table Service Sequence

Department / Area Front of House · FOH Service
Prepared By FOH Manager, General Manager
Date Reviewed May 2026
Version 1.0

Table Service Sequence defines the required standard, sequence, and controls for this procedure.

Key Actions

  • Approach within 2 minutes.
  • Take drinks first and set expectations.
  • Take food orders with modifiers confirmed.
  • Check back after first bites.
  • Clear, offer next step, and close cleanly.
1

Purpose

This SOP defines the required standard for table service sequence so the team follows the same safe, consistent process every shift.

2

Scope

Applies to Server and any team member assigned to this procedure, related checks, or manager follow-up.

3

Responsibilities

  • All assigned staff must follow this SOP exactly as written during the applicable task or shift.
  • The responsible manager or team (FOH Manager, General Manager) is responsible for training staff, answering questions, and verifying that the procedure is followed.
  • The shift lead or manager must stop the task and start corrective action when the standard cannot be met.
4

Definitions / Key Terms

TermDefinition
SOPStandard Operating Procedure; the approved method for completing this task.
Person in ChargeThe manager or certified lead responsible for supervising the task and making food safety decisions during the shift.
Corrective ActionThe required response when a step, limit, record, or safety standard is missed or cannot be verified.
5

Materials / Equipment Needed

  • Approved tools, equipment, utensils, and supplies required for the task.
  • Required personal protective equipment, cleaning supplies, labels, logs, or checklists.
  • Working hand sink, sanitizer, thermometer, or other food safety control equipment when applicable.
6

Procedure (Step-by-Step)

Step 1 - Approach within 2 minutes

Greet the table within 2 minutes of seating. Introduce yourself, offer water, explain specials briefly, and ask about allergies before taking any order. If you are delayed, ask another server or the floor lead to greet them.

Step 2 - Take drinks first and set expectations

Take drinks promptly, repeat the order, and enter it before taking another table’s food order. If the bar is backed up, tell the guest early rather than waiting until they ask.

Step 3 - Take food orders with modifiers confirmed

Ask for cooking temperatures where needed, confirm sides, note allergies in the POS, and repeat anything modified. Do not promise substitutions without kitchen approval. Fire courses according to the manager’s pacing standard.

Step 4 - Check back after first bites

Check back within 2 minutes or 2 bites after food lands. Ask a specific question: “Is the chicken cooked how you like it?” Fix problems immediately and involve the manager when food, allergies, timing, or guest tone requires it.

Step 5 - Clear, offer next step, and close cleanly

Clear only when everyone who is still eating is comfortable. Offer dessert, coffee, another drink, or the bill. Before payment, check the bill for correct table, discounts, comps, and split requests.

7

Safety & Compliance Considerations

Corrective Action

If the standard cannot be met

Stop the task, tell the manager, isolate any affected food, equipment, or records, and follow the corrective action process before resuming.

If food safety may be affected

Do not serve or store the affected item until a manager confirms the next action. Discard when safety cannot be verified.

8

Quality Control / Verification

Follow Sequence Complete steps in order
Stop If Unsure Ask the manager before guessing
Keep Clean Reset tools and surfaces after use
9

Documentation & Records

Record any failed check, corrective action, manager decision, or required sign-off before the end of the shift.
