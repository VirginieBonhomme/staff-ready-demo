What You Need to Know
Non-Negotiable

Food Safety First

Handwashing, glove use, allergen awareness, and temperature checks are required before anything else.

 Every Shift

Use the Checklist

Do not rely on memory for opening. Work through the checklist and submit the form when done.

 Critical

Allergen Tickets

If a guest has an allergy, stop and follow the SOP. Never guess or promise something is safe.

 When in Doubt

Escalate, Don’t Guess

Use the crisis reference for any emergency. If you are not sure what to do, ask your manager first.
